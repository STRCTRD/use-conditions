Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

STRCTRD is a mobile application designed to explore and visualize biological molecular structures, including proteins, DNA and RNA.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use STRCTRD.

1. Information We Collect

STRCTRD does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to access its features.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses or account credentials. It does not integrate advertising or analytics tracking services.

2. Locally Stored Information

Files imported by users, including PDB files, are stored locally on the user's device.

These imported files are not uploaded to STRCTRD servers.

Application preferences and recently viewed structures may also be stored locally on the device.

3. Third-Party Scientific Databases

STRCTRD connects to external scientific databases to retrieve molecular structures and related scientific information.

These services include:

  • RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB)
  • Electron Microscopy Data Bank (EMDB)

When communicating with these external services, technical information such as the device's IP address may be transmitted as part of standard internet communication.

These third-party services operate independently and are subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Advertising and Analytics

STRCTRD does not display advertisements and does not integrate advertising or analytics tracking services.

5. Data Storage and Security

User-imported molecular structure files remain on the user's device.

STRCTRD does not operate a personal data storage server for user-imported files.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and locally stored files.

6. Children's Privacy

STRCTRD does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights regarding personal information processed about you.

For questions or requests concerning privacy, please contact us using the email address below.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if application features or data handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published at this same URL.
